## S. Policy Schedule
Policy No. GCI/MOT/2025/778120. Insured vehicle: private sedan, registration TS 09 AB 4821. Owner: Ravi Kumar. Insured declared value (IDV): ₹6,50,000. Annual premium: ₹14,200. The policy expires on 20 November 2026 and renewal is due by that date.

## 2.1 Own Damage Cover
Loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accident, fire, explosion, theft, riot, flood, earthquake and other natural calamities is covered up to the IDV.

## 2.2 Third Party Liability
Liability for death or bodily injury to a third party is unlimited. Third party property damage is covered up to ₹7,50,000.

## 2.3 Owner-driver Personal Accident
Personal accident cover of ₹15,00,000 applies to the owner-driver for death or permanent total disability caused by an accident while driving the insured vehicle.

## 3.1 Deductibles
A compulsory deductible of ₹1,000 applies to every own damage claim. A voluntary deductible of ₹2,500 has also been opted for, which reduces the premium.

## 3.2 No Claim Bonus
A discount of 20% on own damage premium is earned after the first claim free year, rising to a maximum of 50% after five claim free years. The bonus is lost if a claim is made.

## 4.1 Add-ons
Zero depreciation and roadside assistance add-ons are included. Engine protection and consumables cover are not included.

## 4.2 Claim Procedure
The insurer must be informed within 24 hours of the accident. A police FIR is mandatory for theft, third party injury and riot damage. The vehicle must be inspected by the insurer's surveyor before repair.

## 5.1 Exclusions
The policy does not cover damage when the driver has no valid driving licence or is driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, normal wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, consequential loss, and war.

## 5.2 Lapse and No Claim Bonus
If the policy is not renewed within 90 days of expiry, the accumulated no claim bonus is lost.
