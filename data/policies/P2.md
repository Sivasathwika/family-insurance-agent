## S. Policy Schedule
Policy No. SHI/SC/2025/001873. Insured person: Venkata Rao, aged 82. Policyholder: Ravi Kumar. Sum insured: ₹5,00,000 on an individual basis. Annual premium: ₹41,800 including GST. The next renewal is due on 10 February 2027.

## 1.1 Definitions
Hospitalisation means admission as an inpatient for at least 24 consecutive hours, except for day care procedures. Pre-existing disease means any condition diagnosed or treated within 36 months before inception of the first policy.

## 2.1 Hospitalisation Cover
Room, boarding, nursing, ICU, surgeon and anaesthetist fees, medicines and diagnostics during an inpatient stay are covered, including surgery performed during the stay.

## 2.2 Room Rent and ICU Limits
Room rent is capped at ₹4,000 per day and ICU charges at ₹8,000 per day. Higher room categories lead to proportionate reduction of other charges.

## 2.3 Pre and Post Hospitalisation
Medical expenses are covered for 30 days before admission and 60 days after discharge.

## 2.4 Day Care and Domiciliary Treatment
80 listed day care procedures are covered. Domiciliary hospitalisation, meaning treatment at home because the patient cannot be moved, is covered up to ₹25,000 per policy year.

## 3.1 Co-payment
A co-payment of 20% applies to every claim. The insured bears 20% of the admissible claim amount and the insurer pays the remaining 80%.

## 3.2 Cashless and Reimbursement Claims
Planned hospitalisation must be intimated 72 hours in advance, and emergencies within 24 hours of admission. Reimbursement documents must be submitted within 15 days of discharge.

## 3.3 Cataract Sub-limit
Cataract surgery is payable up to ₹40,000 per eye, and only after the specific disease waiting period.

## 4.1 Annual Health Check-up
One preventive health check-up per policy year is covered up to ₹3,000, irrespective of claims.

## 5.1 Initial Waiting Period
No claim is payable for illness during the first 30 days of the policy, except for accidental injury.

## 5.2 Pre-existing Disease Waiting Period
Pre-existing diseases, including diabetes, hypertension and heart disease, are covered after 24 months of continuous coverage.

## 5.3 Specific Disease Waiting Period
Cataract, hernia, joint replacement, prostate conditions and gall bladder stones are covered after 12 months of continuous coverage.

## 6.1 Exclusions
Cosmetic surgery, dental treatment unless caused by accident, hearing aids, spectacles, infertility treatment, injury due to war and self-inflicted injury are not covered.

## 7.1 Lifelong Renewal
The policy is renewable for life. A grace period of 30 days is allowed after expiry, during which no claim is payable.
