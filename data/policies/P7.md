## S. Policy Schedule
Policy No. JCC/CI/2025/002290. Insured persons: Lakshmi Kumar (primary) and Ravi Kumar. Sum insured: ₹25,00,000 per person. Annual premium: ₹22,600. The next renewal is due on 18 January 2027.

## 1.1 Covered Critical Illnesses
The policy covers 34 critical illnesses including cancer of specified severity, first heart attack, stroke, kidney failure, major organ transplant and coronary artery bypass graft surgery.

## 2.1 Lump Sum Benefit
On diagnosis of a covered critical illness, 100% of the sum insured is paid as a lump sum, irrespective of actual hospital expenses. Cover for that person ends after the benefit is paid.

## 2.2 Definition of Cancer
Cancer means a malignant tumour characterised by uncontrolled growth and spread of malignant cells with invasion and destruction of normal tissue. Carcinoma in situ, early stage prostate cancer and skin cancers other than malignant melanoma are excluded.

## 2.3 Survival Period
The benefit is payable only if the insured survives at least 30 days after diagnosis of the covered illness.

## 3.1 Initial Waiting Period
No benefit is payable for any critical illness diagnosed or showing symptoms within the first 90 days of the policy.

## 3.2 Pre-existing Conditions
Illnesses that were pre-existing at inception are excluded for the first 36 months of continuous coverage.

## 4.1 Claim Procedure
The claim must be intimated within 30 days of diagnosis. Required documents are the claim form, diagnostic reports including the histopathology report for cancer, and discharge summaries.

## 5.1 Exclusions
The policy does not cover illness caused by self-inflicted injury, alcohol or drug abuse, HIV related illness, congenital disorders, war or nuclear contamination.
