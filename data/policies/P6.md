## S. Policy Schedule
Policy No. YGT/INT/2026/55021. Insured person: Sathwika. Annual multi-trip policy with a maximum of 45 days per trip. Overseas medical sum insured: USD 50,000. Annual premium: ₹3,850. Renewal is due on 1 June 2027.

## 2.1 Overseas Medical Expenses
Hospitalisation, surgery, doctor and prescription costs for an emergency illness or accident abroad are covered up to USD 50,000. Cashless treatment is arranged through the assistance partner.

## 2.2 Emergency Medical Evacuation
Emergency evacuation to the nearest adequate facility, or repatriation to India, is covered up to USD 100,000.

## 2.3 Trip Cancellation
Non-refundable trip costs are reimbursed up to ₹50,000 if the trip is cancelled because of the insured's illness or the death of an immediate family member.

## 2.4 Baggage Loss and Delay
Loss of checked baggage is covered up to ₹25,000. A baggage delay of more than 12 hours pays a fixed ₹5,000.

## 3.1 Medical Deductible
A deductible of USD 100 applies to each medical claim. For travel to the USA and Canada the deductible is USD 250.

## 3.2 Claims Procedure
The insurer's assistance partner must be informed within 24 hours of hospitalisation. Claim documents must be submitted within 30 days of returning to India.

## 4.1 Commencement of Cover
There is no waiting period. Cover starts when the insured leaves India and ends on return.

## 4.2 Exclusions
Adventure sports such as skiing, scuba diving and paragliding are excluded unless the adventure add-on is purchased. Pregnancy, mental illness, travel against medical advice and travel to countries under a government travel ban are also excluded.

## 4.3 Pre-existing Conditions
Pre-existing conditions are not covered, except for acute onset of a pre-existing condition up to USD 5,000.
