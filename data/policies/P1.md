## S. Policy Schedule
Policy No. SHI/FF/2025/004512. Policyholder: Ravi Kumar. Insured persons: Ravi Kumar, Lakshmi Kumar, Sathwika and Arjun. Sum insured: ₹10,00,000 on a family floater basis. Annual premium: ₹32,400 including GST. Policy period runs 16 December to 15 December; the next renewal is due on 15 December 2026.

## 1.1 Definitions
Hospitalisation means admission to a hospital as an inpatient for at least 24 consecutive hours, except for day care procedures. Pre-existing disease means any condition, ailment or injury diagnosed or treated within 48 months before the first policy inception.

## 2.1 Hospitalisation Cover
The policy pays room, boarding and nursing charges, ICU charges, surgeon, anaesthetist and specialist fees, medicines, consumables and diagnostics incurred during an inpatient stay. Surgery performed during a covered hospitalisation is payable within the sum insured.

## 2.2 Room Rent and ICU Limits
Room rent is capped at 1% of the sum insured per day, which is ₹10,000 per day. ICU charges are capped at 2% of the sum insured per day, which is ₹20,000 per day. If the insured opts for a higher room category, all other charges are reduced in the same proportion.

## 2.3 Pre and Post Hospitalisation
Medical expenses are covered for 60 days before admission and 90 days after discharge, provided they relate to the same illness or injury.

## 2.4 Day Care Procedures
140 listed day care procedures, such as cataract surgery, dialysis and chemotherapy, are covered even when the stay is shorter than 24 hours.

## 2.5 Maternity Cover
Normal delivery is covered up to ₹50,000 and caesarean delivery up to ₹75,000 per delivery, subject to the 24 month maternity waiting period. A newborn is covered from birth for up to 90 days.

## 2.6 Ambulance Charges
Road ambulance charges are reimbursed up to ₹3,000 per hospitalisation.

## 3.1 Cashless Claims
For planned hospitalisation, the insurer must be informed at least 48 hours in advance. In an emergency, intimation is required within 24 hours of admission. Cashless is available only at network hospitals.

## 3.2 Reimbursement Claims
Claim documents must be submitted within 15 days of discharge. The insurer settles the claim within 30 days of receiving the last necessary document.

## 4.1 Restoration of Sum Insured
If the sum insured is exhausted during the policy year, 100% of it is restored once per year. Restoration cannot be used for the same illness for which the claim was made.

## 4.2 No Claim Bonus
The sum insured increases by 10% for every claim free year, up to a maximum of 50% of the base sum insured.

## 5.1 Initial Waiting Period
No claim is payable for any illness during the first 30 days of the policy. This does not apply to accidental injuries.

## 5.2 Pre-existing Disease Waiting Period
Pre-existing diseases, including diabetes and hypertension, are covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage.

## 5.3 Specific Disease Waiting Period
Treatment for cataract, hernia, hysterectomy, joint replacement unless caused by accident, gall bladder stones, kidney stones and piles is covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage.

## 5.4 Maternity Waiting Period
Maternity expenses are payable only after 24 months of continuous coverage.

## 6.1 Permanent Exclusions
The policy does not cover cosmetic or plastic surgery, dental treatment unless caused by accident, obesity treatment, infertility treatment, experimental treatment, self-inflicted injury, injury due to war, and non-medical consumables.

## 6.2 Co-payment
No co-payment applies to insured persons aged 60 or below.

## 7.1 Renewal and Grace Period
The policy can be renewed within a grace period of 30 days after expiry. No claim is payable for events during the grace period, but continuity of waiting period credit is preserved if the policy is renewed within the grace period.
