## S. Policy Schedule
Policy No. JLI/TS/2024/009921. Life assured: Ravi Kumar. Nominee: Lakshmi Kumar. Sum assured: ₹1,00,00,000. Annual premium: ₹18,900. Policy term 30 years. The next premium is due on 5 March 2027.

## 2.1 Death Benefit
If the life assured dies during the policy term, the full sum assured of ₹1,00,00,000 is paid to the nominee. The nominee may choose a lump sum or a monthly income option spread over 10 years.

## 2.2 Accidental Death Benefit Rider
An additional sum of ₹50,00,000 is paid if death is caused by an accident and occurs within 180 days of the accident.

## 2.3 Terminal Illness Benefit
On diagnosis of a terminal illness with life expectancy of less than 6 months, 50% of the sum assured is paid in advance and deducted from the death benefit.

## 3.1 Premium Payment and Grace Period
A grace period of 30 days is allowed for annual premium payment. If the premium is not paid by the end of the grace period the policy lapses. A lapsed policy can be revived within 2 years on submission of health proof and payment of overdue premiums with interest.

## 4.1 Death Claim Procedure
The nominee must submit the claim form, original policy document, death certificate, nominee identity proof and, for unnatural death, the FIR and post-mortem report. The insurer settles a valid claim within 30 days of receiving all documents.

## 5.1 Suicide Exclusion
If the life assured dies by suicide within 12 months of policy inception or revival, the nominee receives only 80% of the premiums paid, not the sum assured.

## 5.2 Accidental Death Rider Exclusions
The accidental death rider does not pay for death caused by hazardous sports, driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, war, or criminal acts of the life assured.

## 5.3 Contestability and Non-disclosure
The insurer can repudiate the policy for fraud or misstatement of material facts only within 3 years of issuance or revival, as set out in Section 45 of the Insurance Act.

## 6.1 Change of Nominee
The nominee can be changed at any time by a written request to the insurer; the change takes effect once an endorsement is issued on the policy.
