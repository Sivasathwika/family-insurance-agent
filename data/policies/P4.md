## S. Policy Schedule
Policy No. JLI/EP/2018/003340. Life assured: Lakshmi Kumar. Nominee: Ravi Kumar. Basic sum assured: ₹5,00,000. Annual premium: ₹60,000. Policy term 20 years, maturing on 28 November 2038. The annual premium falls due on 28 November; the next due date is 28 November 2026.

## 2.1 Death Benefit
On death during the policy term, the nominee receives the highest of: the sum assured plus accrued bonuses, 105% of all premiums paid, or 10 times the annual premium.

## 2.2 Maturity Benefit
On survival to the maturity date, the policyholder receives the sum assured plus vested reversionary bonuses and any final additional bonus.

## 2.3 Bonus
Reversionary bonuses are declared every year from the insurer's surplus. Bonuses are not guaranteed.

## 3.1 Premium Payment and Grace Period
A grace period of 30 days is allowed for annual premium payment. If the life assured dies during the grace period, the death benefit is paid after deducting the unpaid premium.

## 3.2 Lapse and Paid-up Policy
If premiums have been paid for at least 3 full years and later premiums stop, the policy becomes paid-up with a reduced sum assured. If fewer than 3 full years of premiums were paid, the policy lapses.

## 3.3 Surrender
The policy can be surrendered after 3 full years of premium payment. The guaranteed surrender value is 30% of premiums paid, excluding the first year premium and taxes.

## 3.4 Policy Loan
A loan of up to 80% of the surrender value can be taken against the policy once it has acquired a surrender value.

## 5.1 Suicide Exclusion
If the life assured dies by suicide within 12 months of inception or revival, only 80% of the premiums paid is payable.
